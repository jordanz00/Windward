# /ceo

Have the CEO review the business and establish priorities.

## Steps

1. Read `company/CHARTER.md`, `ORGANIZATION.md`, `STRATEGY.md`, `APPROVAL_MATRIX.md`, `ROADMAP.md`.
2. Skim `company/executives/`, `company/reports/`, and outstanding risks in `company/governance/RISK_REGISTER.md`.
3. Optionally consult SVP domains (do not invent their metrics).
4. Produce: executive summary, top priorities (≤3), owners, risks, decisions required from founder.
5. Do not deploy, spend, or change ownership.
