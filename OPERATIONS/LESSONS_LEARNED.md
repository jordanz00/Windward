# Lessons learned

Studio-wide notes. Project-specific detail stays in each `PROJECTS/…` review or profile; distill reusable lessons here.

## From Windward (001)

- **Single-file procedural demos** read as art when the constraint is honest (no assets, one HTML).  
- **Load/WebGPU success is non-negotiable** for live Pages experiments — file edits alone never prove a ship.  
- **Founder as director, AI as hands** works when plans are bounded and publish stays gated.  
- **Portfolio framing beats company framing** for this work: show the island, don’t invent a department chart.
