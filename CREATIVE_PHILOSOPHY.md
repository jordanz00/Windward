# Creative philosophy

## Stance

This studio is a **lab**. Experiments are made to be seen, felt, and learned from — not to become a company by default. A project earns its place by being interesting, coherent, and finished enough to show, not by having a market slide.

## Roles

- **Founder** sets direction, taste, and hard stops. Approves plans, publishes, and any change that could break what already works.
- **AI** proposes, researches, implements, documents, and presents. It does not invent pass results, skip gates, or ship without approval when the workflow requires it.

## Taste over theater

Prefer one clear idea executed well over feature lists. Prefer real visual / interactive anchors over abstract decks. Prefer short honest reviews over status theater.

## Constraints that sharpen work

- **Bounded v1.** Every plan names what ships in the first cut and what waits.
- **Stability first** on live experiments (especially Windward): load and render success beat speculative polish.
- **Docs and management may move freely; app source moves only under an approved brief.**
- **No false claims.** Unverified work is labeled unverified.

## Portfolio mindset

Projects are entries in a creative portfolio. Completing one means: brief fulfilled, demo-able result, showcase written, lessons captured. Commercialization is optional and separate — never the default frame for studio work.

## Anti-patterns

- Treating the lab as a business with departments and productize phases  
- Rewriting working experiments “for structure” without a creative reason  
- Expanding scope mid-build without a new approved plan  
- Shipping unverified graphics or claiming success from file edits alone
