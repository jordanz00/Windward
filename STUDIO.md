# AI Creative Studio

Independent AI creative lab. Portfolio of experiments — not a company, not a product org, not Windward Inc.

**Founder** = creative director and final taste.  
**AI** = research, planning, implementation under approval gates.  
**Output** = finished experiments, showcases, and lessons — not decks or business plans.

## What this is

A reusable operating system for making things with AI: small creative projects with a clear brief, a bounded build, a public showcase, and a written review. Windward is Project 001 — the first piece in the portfolio, not the studio’s brand identity.

## What this is not

- Not a startup playbook, company charter, or department org chart  
- Not a commercial roadmap or productize-first framing  
- Not permission to rewrite shipped app source without an approved brief  

Discard any prior “Windward 2.0 / BUSINESS / DEPARTMENTS” framing. Studio structure lives here instead.

## Daily loop (founder)

1. `/studio-report` — where things stand  
2. `/new-project` or pick from [OPERATIONS/IDEAS_BACKLOG.md](OPERATIONS/IDEAS_BACKLOG.md)  
3. `/research` → `/plan` → approve → `/build`  
4. `/review` → `/showcase` → `/publish` only when you say so  

Approvals required before: destructive changes, architecture rewrites, deploys/publish, secret use, license or distribution changes.

## Windward note

App source (`source/`, `index.html`, live Pages) stays intact unless a separate, approved project brief says otherwise. Studio docs manage the lab; they do not rewrite the island.
